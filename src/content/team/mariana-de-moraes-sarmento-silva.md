Mariana de Moraes Sarmento Silva is a Research Intern at the Brain in Action Lab, where she contributes to studies on counterfactual memory and decision-making. Beyond her work at the lab, she is engaged in interdisciplinary research in the field of human cognition, contributing to projects that examine psychological, behavioural, and neuroscientific processes. She is currently pursuing a BSc in Psychology with Cognitive Neuroscience at Birkbeck, University of London.
